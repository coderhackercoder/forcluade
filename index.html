Our perspective

Most ‘clean’ labels don't mean what you think.

“Clean,” “natural,” and “non-toxic” aren't regulated terms. Brands can use them without proof. We don't care about marketing. We care about ingredients, sourcing, and transparency.

Our standard is strict by design. If a product contains a blocked ingredient, we don't list it — no matter the brand, price, or popularity.
