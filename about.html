We started NonTox because we got tired of not knowing.

So we built the thing we wished existed.

Why we started

We fell for greenwashing too. A leaf on the label, the word "natural" in size 40 font, a soft green palette — we bought it, literally, for years. It took an unrelated health scare and way too many hours on ingredient databases to realize how much of what we trusted was marketing dressed as safety.

The more we learned, the angrier we got. Not at any one brand — at how normal it is for an industry to regulate itself with words that mean nothing. "Clean." "Non-toxic." "Natural." None of them are defined by law. Anyone can print them on anything.

NonTox is our attempt to fix that — not by scoring products on a curve, but by holding every single one to the same hard line, and telling people plainly when a product doesn't meet it. If that means a beloved brand doesn't make the list, it doesn't make the list.

Who we are

Maya Alvarez, Co-founder

"I wanted to buy my kid a shampoo without reading a chemistry textbook first. That's the whole reason this exists."

NonTox is becoming the place people check before they buy — not after something goes wrong. We're expanding categories carefully, adding food and supplements only once we trust our own standard for it, and keeping the list free and ad-free the whole way.
